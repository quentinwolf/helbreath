          Controls
          Click a door or NPC to select it. Drag (any button) to pan, wheel to zoom, Esc cancels, Ctrl+S saves. In Edit tiles, click or drag to add or remove tiles (right-drag pans). In Edit walkability, click or drag to paint and Shift+drag for a rectangle (right-drag pans). Blocked tiles: red as in the map file, orange newly blocked, green newly walkable. In Edit spawns, drag an NPC or a spawn area to move it, and press Delete to remove the selected spawn. Spawn colours: blue town NPCs, green guards, purple other standing NPCs, orange monsters. Ctrl+Z undoes your last change on any map. Circles are arrival spots, with an arrow for the way you face. Click a section's title to fold it away.
